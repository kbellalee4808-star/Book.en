Step by step

The booking flow

In the same order the screens appear. Checkout runs on a timer, so have passport details ready before you start.

01

Search for a sailing

Search by region and dates. Each card shows the ship, departure port, nights and starting price per person. Tap the title of the one you want.

02

Open the sailing and compare cabins

The top of the page confirms ship, port and dates. Rooms Starting From compares inside, oceanview, balcony and suite. Ready? Tap Book Cruise.

03

Check the itinerary and the ship

Look at the port map and the arrival and departure times before you book. Short port calls leave little margin for booked tours.

04

Confirm passengers (Step 1)

Enter adults, children and seniors. Age is calculated on the boarding date, and travel companions who are not members go here too.

TIPChanging the passenger count recalculates the fare. Get it right before you go further.

05

Choose a cabin category (Step 2)

Pick inside, oceanview, balcony or suite. Within a category you will see sub-types, each with its own starting price.

06

Pick the fare

Same category, different price: it depends on the cabin code (BA, BB…) and the deck. Read the price and deck together, then Select.

07

Choose deck and cabin number (Step 3)

Once you pick a deck, the plan lets you choose the actual cabin. Cabins directly above or below lounges and elevators are the noisiest.

08

Choose how to pay (Step 4)

This is the fork. INsider Pricing pays the discounted price outright. Reward Points offsets up to half the price with the points you hold.

TIPPoints require active status. Check your status and balance before you start a booking.

09

Apply your Reward Points

On a $9,367.63 retail price, the maximum split is 4,683.81 RP + $4,683.82. Points cover half; the rest goes on your card.

10

Split the points between members

Several members can pool points into one booking. Use Add Member, then type how many points each one contributes.

TIPIf the totals do not match, Next will not advance. Adjust the numbers.

11

Enter passenger details (Step 5)

Names and dates of birth must match the passport exactly. Then fill in postal code, country of residence and dining preference.

12

Confirm the passenger list

Once you tick the boxes here, passengers cannot be changed. Read the spelling and dates one more time, then Confirm.

13

Decide on refund protection

The last screen offers refund protection. If your reason is covered by the terms, the amount you paid by card is refunded in full.

TIPCheckout is timed. Having your details ready makes the whole flow comfortable.

Before you book

Have this ready

  • Your membership is active, and you know your points balance
  • Every passenger’s passport spelling and date of birth
  • Anyone pooling points with you has agreed in advance
  • A card that can cover the cash half of the retail price
  • Flights booked with room to spare after disembarkation
